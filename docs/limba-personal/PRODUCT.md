# Product
## Register
product
## Users
Amit and Neta, adult Hebrew speakers studying Romanian in a small online class toward B1 in March 2027. Neta needs an immediately understandable mobile flow. Both use downloaded lesson audio outdoors to avoid cellular costs.
## Product Purpose
Extend the existing digital book with private sign-in and an active personal study companion. One recommended session, adjustable duration, direct topic links, voice-first practice. No registration or invented learning achievements.
## Brand Personality
Calm, adult, encouraging. Continue the existing book's cream, navy and restrained gold language. The user explicitly accepted this direction in the conversation and requested an interactive demonstration.
## Anti-references
No competitive leaderboard, streak pressure, giant readiness percentage, decorative dashboard charts, public social profiles, or forced replacement of book navigation.
## Design Principles
Preserve Claude's content pipeline. Separate publication data from learner events. Never infer mastery from reading. Distinguish classroom coverage from supplemental material. Keep audio downloads prominent and honest.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Hebrew RTL and Romanian LTR; visible labels, keyboard operation, generous mobile targets, reduced motion, text alongside status colors.
